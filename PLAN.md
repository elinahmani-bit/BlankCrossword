# Blank Crossword Scratchpad

## Background

Occasionally defintions for a blank crossword puzzle are published in a newsletter.
The goal of this project is to develop a web app that present a scratchpad of the puzzle
and enable a user to solve the crossword by filling cells with letters.

## Requirements

1. The crossword scratchpad is for the Hebrew language.
2. The app will show an empty crossword, 12x12, on the right side of the window.
3. The lines will be "numbered" by the Hebrew letters Alef to Lamed, on the right side of the crossword.
4. The columns will be numbered, from right to left, by the numbers 1 to 12, on top of the crossword.
5. The cells will accept only Hebrew letters, while a Dot (.) will denote the end of a word by making it black.
6. A current line or column will be bolded by a shadow bar.
7. Clicking the Space bar will switch between line or column.
8. Once a cell is filled with a letter, the cursor wil be advanced to the left or to thr bottom accordingly.
9. On the left side of the window wil be an input box to paste a screen shot of the definitions taken from another window.
